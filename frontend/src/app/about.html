About ApplyCraft

A clearer path to your next application.

ApplyCraft is being built to help you adapt your CV to a specific vacancy and prepare an optional motivation letter, using your actual experience as the starting point.

  1. 01

    Add your CV and the job description.

    Bring your experience and the vacancy together in one workspace.

  2. 02

    Review and edit your application documents.

    Check the wording, make it your own, and keep every detail accurate.

  3. 03

    Download your documents when ready.

    Give your CV and optional letter a final review before exporting.

Your experience. Your final say.

You review the final wording before using your documents. ApplyCraft should never invent qualifications, skills, or experience.

Signed-in users can save application drafts and return to edit them later.
