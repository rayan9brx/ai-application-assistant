## 5. Uploaded CVs and job descriptions
When you upload a CV or paste text or a job description, we process the content you provide. This may include your name, contact details, education, work experience and skills. We store your CVs, analysis results and score history in your account so you can access them later.
**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
**Storage period:** until you delete the CV or your account. Original files are not stored permanently; only the extracted text and results are saved.
Please only upload content you are entitled to use, and avoid special categories of personal data (e.g. health information) that are not needed for a CV.

## 6. AI analysis (Anthropic)
To analyse and improve your CV, we send the CV text and, if provided, the job description to the AI service of **Anthropic PBC** (548 Market Street, PMB 90375, San Francisco, CA 94104, USA). Anthropic processes the data on our behalf to generate the analysis and returns the result to us. According to Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent via the API is not used to train its models.
This involves a transfer of data to the USA. The transfer is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
The AI results (scores, suggestions) are automated suggestions for your own use. They do not constitute a decision with legal effect on you within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

## 7. Website analytics (without cookies)
We measure the use of our website with our own privacy-friendly analytics, **without cookies and without third-party tools**. We record the page visited, the time, the referring website (domain only), the device type (mobile/desktop) and an approximate location (country and city). The location is derived from your IP address at the moment of the visit; **the IP address itself is not stored**. To count unique visitors, we use an anonymous identifier that changes every day and cannot be traced back to you.
**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in understanding and improving our service).
**Storage period:** 12 months.

## 8. Usage data of AI features
For billing, abuse prevention and cost control, we log for each AI request the time, the feature used and the number of processed tokens, linked to your account.
**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
**Storage period:** 12 months, or until your account is deleted.
